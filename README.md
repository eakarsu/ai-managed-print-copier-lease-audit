# Managed Print & Copier Lease Audit

Audit device leases, meter usage, included allowances, supplies, service credits, and end-of-term charges.

**Primary buyer:** Enterprises, schools, and healthcare systems. **Evidence:** lease agreements, devices, meter reads, included volumes, click rates, supplies, service events, relocations, terminations, invoices, and credits.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Lease agreement library
- Device serial registry
- Meter read ingestion
- Included volume calculation
- Mono color click rate
- Supply inclusion control
- Service downtime credit
- Device relocation tracking
- Duplicate device billing
- Property tax fee review
- Early termination audit
- Invoice recalculation
- Vendor dispute workflow
- Credit reconciliation
- Fleet device analytics
